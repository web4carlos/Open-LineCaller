# CP-0035.9.4.2 RC5 — Local Flight Transition Continuity

RC5 addresses the RC4 visual failure: the old local path lost the ball after a paddle hit and the engineering gate later performed a global reacquisition that could attach the marker to the wrong object.

## Locked contract

- A direction-changing hit terminates the **old path**, not the **BallSession**.
- The last trusted `P(x,y,z,t)` remains the transition anchor.
- During the transition bridge the old velocity vector is **not** used to aim the search.
- The same locked Ball Identity is checked only in a small local 3-D Ball-Unit neighborhood around the last trusted observation.
- No global reacquisition is allowed in the transition bridge.
- Each accepted observation becomes the next local anchor.
- Three accepted post-transition observations provide the minimum window to fit `Vnew=(vx,vy,vz)` and start the new continuous path.
- DCF cells are only the local search discretization. They do not define the curve.
- Production DCF/mesh remains invisible.

RC5 is an engineering gate. It is not committed/tagged until the marker is visually confirmed to remain on the real ball across the paddle-hit transition.
