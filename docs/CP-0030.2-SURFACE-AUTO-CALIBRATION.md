# CP-0030.2 — Surface-Aware Auto Calibration

Why this exists:
Line-only auto calibration remained fragile on the night pickleball video.

New strategy:
1. Detect the blue/cyan playing surface.
2. Extract the largest plausible trapezoidal court region.
3. Order corners:
   near-left, near-right, far-right, far-left.
4. Use edge/line evidence only as a secondary support score.
5. Reject implausible geometry safely.

AUTO remains first/default.
MANUAL remains fallback.
