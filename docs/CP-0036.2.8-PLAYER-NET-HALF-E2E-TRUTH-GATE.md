## Net-post perspective correction

The E2E gate exposes an important difference between the old Facilities
camera and Player.

With a high/broadcast camera, a physically descending ball commonly moves
down in the image. CP-0036.2.4.5 used that image-DOWN evidence as an extra
approach guard.

With the approved Player camera mounted low at the net post, the ground-plane
motion from the net toward the far baseline can dominate the image projection.
The ball can therefore be physically descending while its image centroid moves
up the screen.

For Player only, when `trajectory_net_ingress_guard` is active,
CP-0036.2.8 disables that broadcast-specific image-DOWN predicate.

The remaining official chain is still strict:

- selected ball color;
- real rendered image component;
- net-band acquisition;
- inward motion;
- trajectory identity / Top-3;
- perspective scale;
- projected Z0 signature;
- approach continuity/prediction;
- outside-grid geometry;
- later UP confirmation.

Facilities/FULL behavior is unchanged because its Net-Ingress guard is OFF.
