# CP-0007.1 User Guide

## Point order

1. Bottom-left outer corner
2. Bottom-right outer corner
3. Top-right outer corner
4. Top-left outer corner
5. Bottom-left Kitchen corner
6. Bottom-right Kitchen corner
7. Top-left Kitchen corner
8. Top-right Kitchen corner

Always click the centerline intersection of the painted court lines.

## Magnifier

Move the mouse over the video. The magnifier shows a zoomed crop around the
cursor with a crosshair.

## Dragging

After placing a point, click and drag near it to refine its position.

## Per-point error

After Compute Calibration, each point shows its reprojection error.

- green: <= 2 px
- yellow: > 2 and <= 4 px
- red: > 4 px

A single red point is often enough to explain an INVALID calibration.
