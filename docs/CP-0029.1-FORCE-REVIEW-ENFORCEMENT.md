# CP-0029.1 — Force Review Enforcement

This is the final safety layer between CP-0029 geometric decisions and
officiating output.

Rules:
- VERIFIED_BOUNCE: preserve base IN / OUT / REVIEW.
- REVIEW_BOUNCE: force final REVIEW.
- force_review=True: force final REVIEW regardless of geometry.
- Unknown or unsafe gate state: fail safe to REVIEW.

This guarantees that weak bounce evidence cannot silently become a definitive
automatic call later in the pipeline.
