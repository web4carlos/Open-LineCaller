# CP-0032.2 — NVZ / Kitchen + Volley Fault Engine

This capability intentionally avoids the incorrect shortcut:

    PLAYER IN KITCHEN = FAULT

That is not the rule.

The engine distinguishes:

- groundstroke after a bounce
- volley before a bounce
- player touching the NVZ/boundary during a volley
- momentum from a volley carrying the player into the NVZ
- incomplete momentum evidence
- upstream REVIEW uncertainty

Core behavior:

- A bounced ball may be played while the player is in the NVZ.
- A player may enter the NVZ when not violating the volley restriction.
- Volley while touching the NVZ/boundary -> NVZ_FAULT.
- Volley momentum carrying the player into NVZ -> NVZ_FAULT.
- Incomplete momentum evidence -> REVIEW.
- Upstream REVIEW -> REVIEW.

CP-0032.2 is a deterministic rules layer. Player-foot localization and
momentum evidence generation belong to the perception/evidence pipeline.
