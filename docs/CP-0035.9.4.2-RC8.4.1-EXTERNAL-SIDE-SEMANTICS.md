## Purpose

Lock the court-coordinate convention used by the official External Grid.

The External Grid remains floor-only and outside-only.

## Coordinate convention

- Court-space `Y = 0` is the FAR baseline.
- Court-space `Y = court_y` is the NEAR baseline.
- `Y < 0` is `OUT_FAR`.
- `Y > court_y` is `OUT_NEAR`.
- `X < 0` is `OUT_LEFT`.
- `X > court_x` is `OUT_RIGHT`.
- Cells outside two axes remain `OUT_CORNER`.

This prevents a physically FAR impact from being reported as NEAR, or vice versa.

No interior grid is introduced.
No production mesh is rendered.
