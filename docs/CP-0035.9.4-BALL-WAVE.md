# CP-0035.9.4 — DCF Ball Wave / Cell-to-Cell Tracking

## Principle

After CP-0035.9.3 finds one winning DCF cell, LineCaller must not perform a
full Ball-in-Mesh acquisition scan on every live frame.

The winning cell becomes the center of an internal **Ball Wave**.

## Live flow

```text
Global Ball-in-Mesh acquisition
        ↓
Cell(X,Y,Z)
        ↓
Ball Wave
        ↓
local candidate cells only
        ↓
next Cell(X,Y,Z)
        ↓
velocity + uncertainty update
        ↓
next local wave
```

## Dynamic Court Field reuse

CP-0035.9.4 reuses `DynamicCourtField.illuminate_prediction()`:

- base active X/Y/Z radius;
- velocity-forward prediction;
- uncertainty-driven expansion;
- external officiating cells remain valid candidates.

The term "illuminate" here is internal DCF state. The mesh/wave is NOT rendered
in production.

## Miss handling

A missed frame does not mean a new ball.

1. keep the same locked Ball Session;
2. expand wave uncertainty;
3. continue local search;
4. after repeated local misses, request explicit global reacquisition of the
   same locked ball.

A real ball replacement is a different match event. Incrementing the active
ball generation invalidates the old Ball Wave.

## Z=0

CP-0035.9.4 may report:

`contact_plane_reached=True`

when the accepted winning cell has `z == 0`.

It deliberately returns:

`floor_illumination=False`

Floor-cell illumination and contact evidence belong to CP-0035.9.5.

## Production visibility

- DCF: invisible
- Mesh: invisible
- Ball Wave: invisible
- No YOLO
- No hardcoded ball color
- No hardcoded court/camera
