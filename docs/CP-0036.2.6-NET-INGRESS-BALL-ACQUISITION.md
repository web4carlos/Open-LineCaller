# CP-0036.2.6 — Net-Ingress Ball Acquisition

## Product split

LineCaller now treats the two physical products explicitly:

- PLAYER NET HALF — two ordinary phones mounted on the net, one per half-court.
- FACILITY FULL — fixed facility cameras may use FULL_COURT coverage.

FULL_COURT is not removed. It belongs to the Facilities product.

## Player acquisition rule

A Player phone is responsible for only its local half-court. A brand-new ball
trajectory may acquire ownership only when its short motion chain:

1. starts in a broad configurable ingress band next to the local net edge;
2. moves from the net direction into the local half-court;
3. passes the existing selected-ball appearance, receiving-side, perspective
   scale, motion continuity, straightness and prediction gates.

The default ingress band is 36 BU deep from the net edge and allows the
existing 4 BU net margin. The minimum inward progress is 1 BU over the
bootstrap chain.

These values are deliberately configurable because physical phone placement,
lens and elevation will need tuning against real net-mounted footage.

## Fail-closed reacquisition

The ingress gate applies only when the trajectory is UNLOCKED.

Once the ball is locked, normal prediction + Top-3 tracking continues across
the half-court. If the trajectory is lost beyond the existing miss allowance,
a random object deeper in the court cannot immediately become the new ball.
Reacquisition must again satisfy net ingress.

This is a safety feature: uncertainty suppresses OUT rather than inventing a
new identity.

## Facilities isolation

FACILITY FULL does not use the Player net-ingress guard. The existing full
court receiving-half, perspective scale and Top-3 logic remains available for
facility-camera development.

## Geometry invariant

OUT remains OUTSIDE GRID ONLY.

Net ingress uses continuous homography coordinates solely for trajectory
acquisition evidence. It does not create, traverse or depend on an interior
court grid for the line call.

## Telemetry

- BOOT INGRESS REJECTS
- INGRESS START Y BU
- INGRESS END Y BU
- INGRESS INWARD BU
- INGRESS REASON

Rejection reasons include:

- START_NOT_NET_BAND
- NOT_MOVING_INTO_HALF
- NO_FLOOR
- NO_MODEL_OR_CHAIN

Runtime labels are now:

- PLAYER NET HALF
- FACILITY FULL
