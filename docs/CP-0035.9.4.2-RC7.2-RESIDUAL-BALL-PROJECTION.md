# CP-0035.9.4.2 RC7.2 — Residual Ball Projection

## Purpose

RC7.2 converts the already-isolated RC7.1 ball residual into **2-D projection evidence** without requiring the residual to be circular.

The residual may be irregular because of motion blur, pickleball holes, compression, lighting, and subtraction against a background frame. RC7.2 therefore does **not** fit a mandatory circle.

## Contract

- Ball Identity answers **WHO**.
- RC7 / RC7.1 residual answers **PRESENCE**.
- RC7.2 measures the ball projection in the image:
  - intensity-weighted center `(u,v)`;
  - area-equivalent diameter;
  - moment-equivalent major/minor diameters;
  - bounding-box width/height;
  - elongation.
- RC7.2 does **not** claim continuous XYZ.
- RC7.2 does **not** define a flight path.
- RC7.2 does **not** define Z0.
- No perfect-circle assumption is required.

## Why multiple size measures?

A residual is often only a partial projection of the ball. A single bounding circle can be misleading. RC7.2 carries several image-space measurements forward so a later camera/physics resolver can decide how to use them.

The `apparent_scale_px` property is only an image-space summary. It is **not** declared to be the physical diameter of the ball.

## Engineering gate

The real-video gate uses the RC5 user-confirmed same-ball sequence and the existing engineering-only background frame. It overlays:

- the prior RC5 tracked center;
- the RC7.2 residual weighted center;
- the isolated residual enlarged with measured center and component box.

The visual gate asks whether the measured center remains on the real projected ball evidence.

## Production visibility

None. This is engineering evidence only.
