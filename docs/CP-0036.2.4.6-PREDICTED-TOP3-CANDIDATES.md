# CP-0036.2.4.6 — Predicted Top-3 Candidate Selection

The 3668-3682 real-video regression proved that a valid-looking component more
than 50 image pixels away from the measured ball trajectory could independently
build its own short motion history and produce an OUT_FAR call.

The official Wizard/API path now establishes one strict image-space trajectory
before Z0 is allowed. Once locked, LineCaller predicts the next ball position,
keeps at most the three nearest scale-compatible components inside the existing
adaptive continuity radius, and only those components may enter the existing
projected Z0, approach, direction, line-touch, UP and OUT gates.

A recovered contact component cannot bootstrap an independent trajectory lock.
Contact appearance recovery also uses only the locked trajectory prediction.

If the locked ball disappears, prediction may survive for at most two frames,
but a far component is not allowed to take over the lock.

The initial lock requires strict selected-ball-color observations, at least two
prior observations, minimum motion, and short-path straightness >= 0.70.

This remains OUTSIDE GRID ONLY for the line decision. The trajectory selector
operates only in image coordinates and never traverses or decides from an
interior court grid.
