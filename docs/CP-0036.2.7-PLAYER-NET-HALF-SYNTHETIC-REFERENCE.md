# CP-0036.2.7 — Player Net-Half Synthetic Reference Harness

## Purpose

Real phone-on-net footage is still required before PLAYER NET HALF can be
called physically validated. That footage should not block engineering.

This pack adds a deterministic synthetic reference for the real Player
topology:

- one phone attached to the net;
- one local HALF_COURT;
- y=91 BU at the net edge;
- y=0 BU at the baseline;
- new ball trajectories enter from the net and move inward;
- same-color moving distractors can exist deeper in the half;
- OUT remains OUTSIDE GRID ONLY.

Synthetic footage is engineering evidence, not a substitute for real footage.

## Reference sequence

The generated 75-frame sequence contains:

- DEEP_DISTRACTOR_ONLY — coherent same-color motion deep inside the half; it
  must not bootstrap a Player ball track.
- INGRESS_OUT_LEFT — real ball enters from the net and contacts at frame 24
  with floor truth x=-2.2 BU, y=18.9 BU; expected truth is OUT_LEFT.
- INGRESS_IN_COURT — second ingress with safe inside contact at frame 52;
  expected truth is IN.
- DEEP_DISTRACTOR_REACQUIRE — another deep object after track loss; it must
  remain ineligible for new ownership.

The bundle contains background, HALF_COURT calibration, ball template,
truth.json, PNG frames and an MP4 preview when the local OpenCV codec supports
it. The generator falls back to AVI when needed.

The generator first executes a controlled Net-Ingress selector gate. A deep
distractor must stay UNLOCKED and the real net-ingress trajectory must acquire
BOOTSTRAP ownership.

## Boundary

This harness does not alter official line-call geometry. Homography is used to
render known synthetic truth. Official OUT still depends on outside-grid
contact evidence and the existing confirmation chain.
