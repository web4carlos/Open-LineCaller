- the full External Grid;
- the full receiving-side U-shape.

perspective. RC8.3.2 only selects a subset; it does not recalculate geometry.

- FAR_LEFT
- FAR_BASELINE
- FAR_RIGHT

- NEAR_BASELINE
- NEAR_RIGHT

Each zone has a configurable depth in Ball Units.

`FRAME -> PILLOW(current vs calibrated reference) -> SPECIFIC EXTERNAL ROI`
