Status: IMPLEMENTED

## Purpose

Protect live LineCaller officiating when a low/side-mounted camera near the
net or post moves, vibrates, or loses reliable calibration anchors.

## Guard states

### SAFE

The calibrated boundary anchors remain within the strict displacement/error
limits.

`OUT` decisions may proceed.

### MICRO_ADJUST

The camera moved, but the movement is still recoverable with a robust
reference-to-current homography.

`OUT` is temporarily blocked.

The same homography is applied to:

- calibration image points;
- every already-approved External Grid polygon/bbox;
- expected floor ball scale;
- calibrated image-up direction;
- calibrated Pillow/background reference.

After adjustment the guard must evaluate SAFE before officiating resumes.

### BLOCKED

Anchor evidence is insufficient, homography confidence is poor, or displacement
exceeds the recovery threshold.

LineCaller must issue no OUT call until recalibration/recovery.

## Anchor strategy

The guard detects/tracks visual features only in bands around the four
calibrated exterior boundary edges and corners.

It uses:

1. OpenCV good-features-to-track on the calibration reference;
2. pyramidal Lucas-Kanade optical flow into the current frame;
3. RANSAC homography;
4. inlier confidence and reprojection RMSE;
5. displacement of the four official calibration corners.

## Exterior-grid invariant

Pose repair changes only IMAGE geometry.

It never changes:

- top-view/world cell coordinates;
- cell IDs;
- OUT region identity;
- camera ownership;
- the fact that the operational grid is exterior-only.

A pose update therefore cannot create an interior scan cell.

## Fail closed

Weak anchors, occlusion, severe movement, or uncertain pose => BLOCKED.

The guard prefers NO CALL / recalibration over a false OUT.
