# CP-0026.4 — Bounce Evidence Integrity

Rule: Kalman may predict where/when to look for a bounce, but Kalman alone cannot certify physical ball-floor contact.

Classes:
- OBSERVED_CONTACT: raw detector evidence exists at contact.
- INFERRED_CONTACT: real observations bracket contact; position is reconstructed with reduced confidence.
- UNVERIFIED_CONTACT: insufficient visual evidence; blocked from officiating.
