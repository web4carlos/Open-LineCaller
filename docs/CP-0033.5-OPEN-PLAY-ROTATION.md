# CP-0033.5 — Open Play / Player Rotation Engine

Initial policy: FOUR_ON_FOUR_OFF.

Behavior:
- four players form the active doubles group;
- when the game finishes and at least four players are waiting, all four
  active players leave;
- the next four players in FIFO queue enter;
- the four departing players move to the back of the queue;
- fewer than four waiting does not create an incomplete replacement game.

This is deliberately separate from official scoring/rules. Open-play rotation
is a facility/session policy layered on top of GAME_FINISHED.

Future policies can include winners-stay, winners-stay-and-split, skill queues,
multiple courts, and custom club rotation.
