# CP-0036.2.4.5 — Descending Approach Gate

The real 3668-3682 validation produced an audible OUT at frame 3676, but the
winning OUT_FAR cells 449/450 are more than 50 image pixels from the measured
real contact. The measured real contact at frame 3677 is in OUT_LEFT cell 1397,
whose center is only 0.50 px from the measured ball center.

For the official live contact-recovery loop, a candidate that already passed
projected identity and Short Motion Memory must now also have a fitted
pre-contact velocity with a physical DOWN component according to the calibrated
image_up_unit.

Minimum DOWN is intentionally small:

    0.05 * expected_projected_ball_diameter px/frame

This is a direction sanity gate, not a high-speed requirement.

New telemetry:
- DIRECTION REJECTS
- DOWN PX/FRAME
- MIN DOWN PX/FRAME
- approach reason NOT_DESCENDING / NO_DIRECTION

No change to OUTSIDE GRID ONLY, line-touch safety, projected signatures,
tiny-ball raster allowance, UP confirmation, camera ownership, or pose safety.
