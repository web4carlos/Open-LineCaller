# CP-0036.2.7.1 — Correct Net-Mount Camera Geometry

## Purpose

CP-0036.2.7 proved deterministic Player Net-Ingress logic, but its first
synthetic court used a centered camera perspective that did not represent the
physical Player product.

The real Player topology is a normal phone attached directly to the pickleball
net near a post, approximately at net height, looking diagonally into one local
half-court.

CP-0036.2.7.1 corrects the synthetic camera geometry only. It does not change
the official line-call geometry.

## Player camera model

The corrected reference models:

- LOW_NET_POST_OBLIQUE camera geometry;
- one phone at the net rather than above the court;
- one local HALF_COURT;
- a large nearby net edge;
- a compressed far baseline;
- strong diagonal perspective from the camera-side net corner;
- RIGHT and LEFT mirrored net-post configurations.

Default synthetic mount is RIGHT.

Generate the LEFT mirror with:

```powershell
cd C:\olinecaller
.\.venv\Scripts\python.exe .\tools\generate_player_net_half_reference.py `
  --mount-side LEFT
```

## Reference homography

RIGHT net-post points use the existing calibration order
net-left, net-right, baseline-right, baseline-left:

```text
(150, 360)
(940, 530)
(650, 150)
(360, 105)
```

LEFT is the exact horizontal/court-side mirror.

These are engineering camera-model points chosen from the physical mounting
topology. They are not claimed to be a measured calibration of one specific
phone installation. Real phone footage and its real calibration remain the
physical validation gate.

## Invariants

- y=91 BU is the local net edge;
- y=0 BU is the local baseline;
- new ownership must enter from the net;
- deep same-color distractors cannot bootstrap;
- reacquisition fails closed;
- OUT remains OUTSIDE GRID ONLY;
- homography is evidence/rendering support, not an interior decision grid.
